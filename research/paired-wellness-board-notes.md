# Paired Wellness — Pinterest Direction

## Board synthesis

The 23-pin board centers on **pears, orchard life, green landscapes, sunlit foliage, grass texture, and restrained editorial branding**. The strongest images pair organic imperfection with precise layout: pears photographed on striped linen, close crops of clustered fruit, orchard rows, a ladder beneath a tree, outdoor tables under branches, backlit leaves, and palette or stationery studies using acid pear green against deep green, ivory, and pale blue-gray.

| Recurring motif | Visual character | Website translation |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh pears | Imperfect, tactile, sun-warmed, often clustered or casually arranged | Pear silhouette mark, photography masks, paired-circle motifs, “rooted + fresh” microcopy |
| Grass and orchards | Deep perspective, natural texture, quiet abundance | Full-width atmospheric bands, soft green gradients, subtle grain, orchard-row dividers |
| Trees and leaves | Backlit, soft focus, movement, filtered sunlight | Hero halo, botanical shadows, leaf line art, organic section transitions |
| Florals | White and green, restrained rather than romantic or pastel-heavy | Small botanical flourishes, cropped petals, quiet corner ornaments |
| Editorial brand boards | Ivory cards, serif wordmarks, exact spacing, sharp typography | Refined Paired Wellness wordmark and high-contrast editorial headline moments |
| Acid pear green | Optimistic and modern against deep green | Primary accent for highlights, controls, selected states, and icons |

## Premium iOS direction

The redesigned interface should feel **spatial, calm, and tactile**, inspired by premium iOS patterns rather than imitating an Apple product page. The strongest translation is a system of translucent layers, large-radius cards, floating navigation, subtle depth, crisp system typography, short micro-interactions, and generous content breathing room.

| Interface principle | Application |
|---|---|
| Frosted glass | Floating navigation and compact contextual pills with `backdrop-filter` |
| Spatial depth | Soft botanical color fields, restrained shadows, and layered cards rather than hard boxes |
| System clarity | `-apple-system`, BlinkMacSystemFont, and a refined display serif used only for editorial emphasis |
| Large radii | 24–36px cards and 999px pills, balanced by sharp editorial image crops |
| Selection states | Pear-green active indicators, segmented-control behavior, and precise icon sizing |
| Motion | 160–260ms transform/opacity transitions, active-scale feedback, and reduced-motion support |
| Premium restraint | Fewer visible rules, fewer all-caps labels, and more hierarchy through spacing and material |

## Brand language

**Paired Wellness** should communicate that healing and stewardship happen in relationship: body and soul, root and fruit, faith and practice, inside and outside. The name should replace every Annie B Wellness reference while Annie remains the founder and personal voice of the brand.

Source board: https://www.pinterest.com/abpfeifer/branding-look-book/

## Close-up pin observations

The strongest pear photograph uses **unpolished yellow-green fruit, striped ivory linen, and hard late-afternoon sun**, producing crisp leaf-like shadows and tactile folds. This supports authentic texture over glossy stock perfection. A second palette reference combines lime with charcoal, petal ivory, pale yellow, and small coral/pink accents; for the website, those warmer notes should remain extremely sparing so the green botanical identity stays dominant.

The selected pear-on-linen pin was saved locally only as a **generation reference** for lighting, crop, textile texture, and imperfect fruit character. It will not be published directly in the website; the production botanical assets will be original.
