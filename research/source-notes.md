## Annie’s personal testimony

Annie’s published testimony describes acne beginning around age ten, persistent bloating and constipation, painful cycles, and brain fog. She tried dermatology, skincare, food elimination, and other symptom-focused approaches before comprehensive testing helped connect food allergy, gut imbalance, yeast, and hormone-related patterns. Her public message is not that healing was instant, but that prayer, testing, consistent habit change, and clearer root-cause direction helped turn a long struggle into service for others.

> “Our God is not a symptom God. He’s a root cause God.” — Annie’s testimony episode, *Health in the Spirit*

The website should preserve this as **personal testimony and educational encouragement**, not a medical guarantee. Every guide and consultation CTA should carry a concise disclaimer that content is educational and does not replace individualized medical care.

## Content safeguards

The site must avoid diagnosing visitors, promising cures, or presenting personal experience as universal evidence. The intended voice is warm, direct, hopeful, explicitly Christ-centered, and grounded in stewardship: **Jesus first, whole-person context, test rather than guess, and patient consistency**.
