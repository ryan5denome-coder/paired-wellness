# Paired Wellness Editorial Template Analysis

## Sections 1–2: Masthead, Bio, and Gut Introduction

The reference abandons the previous glass-heavy interface in favor of a **quiet luxury editorial system**. The canvas is mostly warm white and very pale blush-gray, with no cards, rounded containers, saturated panels, or heavy shadows. Content is organized by generous whitespace, crisp editorial rules, narrow serif copy blocks, and deliberately asymmetric image placement.

The opening composition uses a small hand-drawn pear branch in the upper-left, a large stacked **Paired Wellness** serif wordmark left of center, the line “Restore the gut. Support the skin.”, and a short Christ-centered paragraph below. A large natural photograph of pears hanging on a branch occupies the upper-right. Its only framing device is a small pale caption tab reading “Paired Wellness Method.”

The next section is split almost evenly: a large monochrome portrait of Annie fills the left half, while the right half uses a small underlined “Bio” label, a two-line serif title (“Health Restoration with Annie DeNome”), a narrow story paragraph, and a small secondary monochrome lifestyle photograph aligned far right. The transition into the following section begins with a fine horizontal rule and the heading “Restore the gut,” set in roman plus italic serif.

The visual principles to carry into the live site are: very large warm-white fields; pale blush section backgrounds; primarily black and charcoal typography; classic high-contrast serif titles with restrained italic emphasis; smaller, lighter sans or serif supporting text; square-corner photography; image captions on simple rectangular tabs; monochrome Annie portraits; and a magazine-grid rhythm rather than app-like floating UI.

Annie photography visible so far includes a large seated portrait in a blazer and a smaller exterior lifestyle image. The existing local portrait is the same source image as the reference’s large portrait and can be rendered in grayscale without creating a duplicate asset.

## Sections 3–4: Restore the Gut and Support the Skin

The “Restore the gut” area behaves like an editorial portfolio grid rather than a typical services section. Three numbered entries are scattered across a spacious pale blush background: a practitioner consultation photograph marked 01; a large gut-health lifestyle image and smaller lemon drink image grouped under 02; and RINGANA plus Crunchi product imagery grouped under 03. Captions sit directly below images in small serif text. There are no borders, cards, icons, buttons, or decorative gradients.

The “Support the skin” heading begins low in the same section, paired with a tall warm-toned beauty portrait. This gives the page a slow narrative transition rather than a hard section boundary. The next band uses a five-image orchard mosaic: a woman among plants, pears on a tree, translucent pear slices, a woman standing in the orchard, and a close detail of fruit and leaves. The grid varies width and height, leaving substantial blank space around and between images.

The reference includes another Annie-adjacent portrait: a close, warm-toned beauty image with eyes closed. Because it may be a mood-board image rather than a verified photo of Annie, the site should not represent it as Annie unless it can be matched to an existing approved asset. The practitioner consultation and product still lifes can be omitted or replaced by copy-led numbered entries; the user explicitly prefers Annie’s real images and wants stock used only when needed. The generated orchard assets already in the project can support the pear story sparingly, but should no longer dominate the brand.

## Sections 5–6: Orchard Narrative, Media Story, and Contact

The orchard mosaic resolves with a short italic statement in the lower-left and two images offset to the upper-right. The next section switches to a split editorial story: a very large behind-the-scenes production photograph occupies the left half; two smaller horizontal production stills sit above a compact text block on the right. A short underlined heading introduces the narrative. This composition can be translated into Paired Wellness with Annie’s real portrait, Annie-and-Ryan podcast photograph, and copy about guiding women through the wellness trail—without using the unrelated production stock.

The final contact section returns to white. A narrow left column contains an underlined “Contact” label, the large serif title “Get in Touch,” and compact email/social details. A wide, square-corner photograph of Annie seated on a staircase fills the right. This staircase photograph appears to be a genuine Annie image embedded in the supplied template and is the strongest new personal asset in the reference. It should be extracted for web use if the screenshot resolution is adequate. The reference also contains a smaller Annie-in-the-orchard image and possibly an Annie beauty portrait, but identity is less certain at the screenshot scale; those should be avoided unless the user supplies the originals.

## Final Direction

The live redesign should preserve the existing four routes but visually behave like a single editorial publication: understated top navigation, warm white/blush surfaces, thin rules, asymmetrical grids, square photography, numbered entries, very limited accent color, and ample negative space. Annie’s verified local portrait should be the primary face image, the Health in the Spirit couple image should support the podcast story, and the staircase image extracted from this user-supplied template should become the contact visual. Existing generated pear/orchard photography may remain only for the brand’s opening pear motif and one quiet supporting field where no Annie image is available.

## Approved Personal Image Inventory

The extracted staircase portrait is clean at 850 × 600 and clearly preserves Annie’s identity, pose, staircase setting, and original neutral lighting. It is suitable as a wide contact image or a cropped supporting panel, but should not be enlarged beyond its natural resolution.

The existing 1067 × 1600 portrait is a strong vertical founder image with a simple pale background and can support both full-color and CSS grayscale treatments. It should become the dominant About image and may appear once on the homepage as a large monochrome editorial portrait. The existing Annie-and-Ryan horizontal portrait remains the appropriate media asset for the Health in the Spirit section. To honor the user’s request, no additional lifestyle stock portraits should be introduced.
